Our story

A neighbourhood restaurant inspired by bold South African flavours.

Foody began as a simple idea: to create a warm, modern dining space where quality ingredients, heritage recipes, and genuine hospitality come together. Every dish celebrates the richness of local produce and the creativity of our kitchen team.

Our mission

To create memorable meals that feel personal, nourishing, and deeply connected to place.

Our values

  • Fresh, seasonal ingredients
  • Thoughtful service
  • Inclusive, welcoming dining
